The Art of the Wall

Raiders ride for your city. Walls are the only thing between them and your people.

  • Build — drag to raise a wall. Sections cost coin by length.
  • Raze — drag over a wall to demolish it. Half the price returns, less its damage.
  • Upgrade — click your castle to grow it. A courtyard complex becomes a walled compound, then a moated city.
  • Ctrl+Z — undo the last section you laid.
  • [ and ] — tilt the view. Lower to see your walls stand.

Cavalry die against walls. Infantry walk through them. Winter multiplies the cost of stone; autumn doubles the harvest.

No civilisation lasts forever. The question is how long.
